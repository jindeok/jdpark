About

Theory, built for practice

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning research and large-scale production systems. I develop search and recommendation models over billion-scale commerce data, combining theoretical foundations in ranking, collaborative filtering, graph learning, and representation learning with the practical demands of high-traffic services.

I approach problems end to end—from problem formulation and model design to scalable deployment and operational automation—balancing methodological rigor with latency, robustness, maintainability, and revenue impact. This combination allows me to reason precisely about algorithms while building systems that remain effective as products, user behavior, and business requirements evolve.

Current work

Industry research

I formulate high-impact learning problems for rapidly evolving commerce scenarios, then translate them into models designed for production-scale operation and material revenue impact. Selected initiatives are outlined below.

NAVER Shopping

Shopping Search Ranking Team · Machine Learning Researcher

2025—Present

Selected work at the intersection of ranking, automation, and structured commerce intelligence.

01

Large-Scale Ranking

Designing search and recommendation ranking models for catalogs containing billions of products, with production constraints treated as first-class modeling requirements.

02

Agentic Workflows & Automation

Operating batch pipelines that extract trends and brands from NAVER's internal knowledge sources and automatically derive service-ready explanatory copy for commerce experiences.

03

Commerce Knowledge Graph

Modeling and constructing a heterogeneous knowledge graph connecting queries, products, brands, and trends to support a major redesign of a primary search-results surface.

Descriptions are intentionally limited to non-confidential, high-level research scope.
